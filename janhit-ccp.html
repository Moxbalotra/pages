Child Safety Standards Policy

Janhit Seva is committed to maintaining a safe and respectful digital environment and to protecting children from exploitation, abuse and harmful activities.

Effective Date: 13 October 2026
Our Commitment

Child safety is a serious responsibility. We do not tolerate child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, or any conduct that sexually exploits or endangers children.

1. Purpose and Scope

This Child Safety Standards Policy applies to the Janhit Seva mobile application and related services operated by Janhit Seva Sansthan, to the extent applicable to their features.

This policy describes our child-safety commitments, prohibited conduct, reporting process and approach to responding to credible child-safety concerns.

2. Zero Tolerance for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation

Janhit Seva prohibits the use of its services to facilitate, promote, distribute or engage in child sexual abuse or exploitation.

Prohibited conduct includes, but is not limited to:

  • Creating, uploading, requesting or distributing child sexual abuse material.
  • Sexual exploitation of children in any form.
  • Grooming, solicitation or sexual harassment of minors.
  • Using the platform to arrange or facilitate the abuse of a child.
  • Threatening, coercing or exploiting children through digital communications.
  • Sharing content or information intended to facilitate child abuse or exploitation.

Violations may result in content removal, restriction or termination of access, preservation of relevant information where lawful, and reporting to appropriate authorities where required by law.

3. Child Safety Prevention Measures

We aim to implement appropriate safeguards according to the features and risks of our application. These measures may include:

  • Providing a channel for users to report child-safety concerns.
  • Reviewing credible reports and taking appropriate action.
  • Restricting accounts or removing prohibited content when warranted.
  • Limiting access to personal information to authorised personnel.
  • Cooperating with lawful requests from competent authorities.
  • Reviewing relevant safety procedures as our services evolve.

The measures implemented in practice will depend on the application's actual features, technical capabilities and applicable legal obligations.

4. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users, parents, guardians and other concerned individuals can report suspected child exploitation, grooming, abusive content or other child-safety violations using the contact details provided below.

When submitting a report, please provide, where safe and appropriate:

  • A brief description of the concern.
  • The relevant account name, profile or publicly accessible content link, if available.
  • The date and general circumstances of the incident.
  • Your contact details if you would like a response.

Do not download, copy, forward or attach suspected child sexual abuse material to a report. Provide identifying information or links only where lawful and safe to do so.

We will review reports as appropriate, prioritise credible urgent concerns, and take suitable action based on the information available.

5. Handling Reports and Enforcement

Where a report raises a credible safety concern, we may take steps appropriate to the circumstances, including:

  • Reviewing the report and relevant available information.
  • Removing or restricting access to violating content where applicable.
  • Suspending or terminating accounts involved in serious violations.
  • Taking steps to prevent further misuse of our services.
  • Escalating matters to law enforcement or child-protection authorities where required or appropriate.

We will handle reports with appropriate discretion and limit access to sensitive information to authorised persons.

6. Cooperation with Authorities

Janhit Seva will respond to valid legal requests and cooperate with competent law-enforcement and child-protection authorities as required by applicable law.

In India, suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation may be reported to the appropriate law-enforcement agency or competent child-protection authority. In an immediate emergency, contact local emergency services.

7. Child Privacy and Personal Information

We aim to handle personal information in accordance with applicable data-protection and child-protection laws. Information submitted in a safety report will be used to assess, investigate and respond to the concern, meet legal obligations, and protect users where appropriate.

We take reasonable steps to prevent unauthorised access to sensitive reports. Information may be disclosed to competent authorities or other relevant parties when legally required or otherwise lawfully appropriate.

For more information about the collection, use, storage and deletion of personal information, please refer to the Janhit Seva Privacy Policy published on our official website.

8. Awareness and Responsible Use

Users must use Janhit Seva responsibly and must not use the platform to threaten, exploit, harass or endanger children. We encourage parents, guardians and caregivers to help children use digital services safely and to report suspected abuse to the appropriate authorities.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our services, safety practices, technical capabilities or applicable law. The latest version will be published on this page along with its effective date.

10. Child Safety Contact

For child-safety reports, policy questions or concerns about the implementation of these standards, contact the organisation responsible for the application.
