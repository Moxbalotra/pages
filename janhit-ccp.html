Child Safety Standards

Janhit Seva Sansthan is committed to maintaining a safe and responsible digital environment. We take child protection seriously and do not tolerate any form of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE).

Effective Date: 14 November 2026 Organisation: Janhit Seva Sansthan Location: India
01 / Our Commitment

Zero Tolerance for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation

Janhit Seva Sansthan maintains a zero-tolerance approach towards Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).

Our Commitment: We prohibit the use of our application and associated services for any activity that exploits, abuses or endangers children.

We aim to promote a safe digital environment through appropriate policies, reporting procedures and responsible handling of child safety concerns.

02 / Prohibited Activities

Activities Strictly Prohibited

The following activities are strictly prohibited on the Janhit Seva platform and in connection with its services:

  • Creating, uploading, sharing or distributing Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).
  • Sexual exploitation or abuse of children.
  • Grooming or attempting to groom a minor.
  • Sexualisation of children.
  • Sextortion or threats involving minors.
  • Trafficking or solicitation of children for sexual exploitation.
  • Encouraging, facilitating or promoting any form of child abuse.

Any violation may result in appropriate action, including content removal, account suspension or termination where applicable, and reporting to relevant authorities.

03 / User Responsibilities

Responsible Use of the Platform

Users are expected to use the Janhit Seva application responsibly and comply with applicable laws and these Child Safety Standards.

  • Do not upload or share content that exploits or endangers children.
  • Report suspected child safety violations.
  • Do not use the platform to contact or exploit minors for inappropriate purposes.
  • Cooperate with legitimate safety reviews and investigations.
04 / Reporting Mechanism

How to Report a Child Safety Concern

We encourage users to report suspected child abuse, exploitation, inappropriate content or any child safety concern associated with the Janhit Seva application.

Reporting Options

  • Use the in-app Report, Help or Contact facility, where available.
  • Send a report to our designated Child Safety Point of Contact using the email address provided on this page.
  • Include relevant details about the concern. Do not download, forward or redistribute suspected illegal material.
Important: Reports involving suspected CSAM or child exploitation will be treated seriously and reviewed in accordance with applicable laws.
05 / Review & Action

Handling of Child Safety Reports

When Janhit Seva Sansthan obtains actual knowledge of suspected CSAM or child exploitation connected with its services, appropriate action will be taken in accordance with applicable laws and organisational procedures.

Such action may include:

  • Reviewing reported safety concerns.
  • Removing or restricting prohibited content.
  • Restricting or terminating violating accounts, where applicable.
  • Preserving relevant information where legally required.
  • Reporting confirmed CSAM to appropriate authorities.
  • Cooperating with lawful investigations.
06 / Legal Compliance

Compliance with Applicable Laws

Janhit Seva Sansthan aims to comply with applicable child protection, online safety and digital platform regulations.

This includes relevant laws in India and other jurisdictions where the application is made available.

Where required by law, suspected or confirmed child exploitation incidents will be reported to appropriate authorities.

07 / Privacy & Protection

Privacy and Confidentiality

Child safety reports and related information will be handled with appropriate confidentiality and access restrictions.

Personal information associated with reports will be processed in accordance with applicable privacy laws and our Privacy Policy.

For further information about data collection, use, retention and deletion, please refer to our Privacy Policy.
