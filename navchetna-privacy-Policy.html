Privacy Policy

Navchetna Charitable Trust

Last Updated: October 22, 2026

Navchetna Charitable Trust ("Trust", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, process, and protect information when you use our mobile application, website, and related services.

By using our services, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. Where consent is legally required, we will request it before carrying out the relevant processing.

1. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on the features available in our application and how you use our services.

  • Personal Information: Name, mobile number, email address, and other details you voluntarily provide.
  • Account Information: Profile details and authentication information, if account registration and login features are available.
  • Donation Information: Donation amount, transaction reference, donor details, and related records, if donation facilities are provided.
  • Photos and Documents: Images, profile photographs, or documents you select and submit through the application, where supported.
  • Communication Information: Messages, enquiries, feedback, complaints, and information provided when contacting us.
  • Technical Information: Device type, operating system, application version, diagnostic logs, and usage information, where collected by our application or service providers.

We collect information relevant to the services we provide. We do not intentionally collect unnecessary personal information. Actual collection depends on the features implemented in the released application.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our services.
  • To create and manage user accounts, where applicable.
  • To respond to enquiries, requests, and feedback.
  • To manage charitable activities, welfare initiatives, events, and community services.
  • To process and maintain donation records, where applicable.
  • To send important service notifications and announcements.
  • To improve application performance and user experience.
  • To protect our services against fraud, misuse, unauthorized access, and security threats.
  • To comply with applicable laws and legal obligations.

3. Device Permissions

Our application may request certain device permissions to provide specific features. Permissions are requested only when relevant to the implemented functionality.

  • Photos and Media: To allow you to select or upload a profile photograph, image, or document when the relevant feature is available.
  • Camera: To capture photographs or documents if supported.
  • Notifications: To send service updates and announcements if notifications are enabled.
  • Location: To provide location-based functionality only if such a feature exists and the required permission is granted.

You can manage available permissions through your device settings. Denying an optional permission may limit the corresponding feature without necessarily affecting the rest of the application.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information when reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including:

  • Service Providers: Authorized hosting, database, technical support, communication, and security providers.
  • Payment Providers: Payment processors that handle transactions when donation or payment facilities are available.
  • Legal Authorities: Where disclosure is required by applicable law, a valid legal process, or a lawful government request.
  • With Consent: Where you have provided appropriate consent for a specific disclosure or purpose.

Service providers may process information on our behalf under their applicable contractual and legal obligations. We aim to use appropriate safeguards when sharing data.

5. Third-Party Services

Our application may rely on third-party services such as hosting providers, analytics tools, payment gateways, crash-reporting services, or notification platforms. These services may process information according to their respective privacy policies and configurations.

Where applicable, information about third-party SDKs and their data practices should also be disclosed in the relevant app store privacy declarations.

6. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, and misuse.

Depending on the services used, information may be stored on our systems or on systems operated by authorized service providers.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, fulfil the purposes described in this policy, resolve disputes, maintain necessary records, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or otherwise dispose of it securely, subject to applicable legal requirements.

8. Account and Data Deletion

If our application supports account creation, users may request deletion of their accounts and associated personal information using the deletion facility provided in the application or by contacting us through the details below.

We will review and process eligible requests in accordance with applicable laws. Certain information may be retained when required for legal compliance, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legitimate record-keeping purposes. Where appropriate, retained information will be restricted from other uses.

If account creation is supported, the application and its associated website must provide the account deletion mechanisms required by the applicable Google Play and Apple App Store rules.

9. Children's Privacy

Our application is not intended to knowingly collect children's personal information without appropriate consent and safeguards required by applicable law.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected inappropriately from a child, we will take reasonable steps to review the situation and address the information in accordance with applicable law. Parents or legal guardians may contact us with relevant privacy concerns.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of eligible personal information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Raise a privacy concern or submit a complaint.

To exercise an applicable right, please contact us using the contact information provided below. We may need to verify your identity before processing a request.

11. Donations and Payment Information

If our services support online donations or payments, transactions may be processed through third-party payment providers. We may receive transaction references, donation amounts, payment status, and relevant accounting records.

Payment card details, banking credentials, and payment authentication information should be handled by the appropriate payment provider. We will not claim to store such information unless our actual systems do so.

12. Data Transfers

Where our service providers store or process information outside your state or country, the information may be subject to the laws applicable in that location. We will take steps required by applicable law to ensure appropriate safeguards for relevant transfers.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, data practices, technology, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. Where legally required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.
