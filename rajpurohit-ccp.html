Child Safety Standards Policy

Rajpurohit Jan Seva Foundation is committed to promoting a safe, respectful and responsible digital environment. We do not tolerate child sexual abuse, exploitation or any conduct that endangers children.

Effective Date: 26 October 2026
Our Commitment to Child Safety

Protecting children is a serious responsibility. Rajpurohit Jan Seva Foundation prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming and other conduct that sexually exploits or endangers children. We aim to address credible reports appropriately and in accordance with applicable law.

1. Purpose and Scope

This Child Safety Standards Policy applies to the digital applications, online services and related activities operated by Rajpurohit Jan Seva Foundation, to the extent applicable to their features and functions.

The purpose of this policy is to explain our child-safety principles, prohibited conduct, reporting procedures, privacy practices and approach to handling credible child-safety concerns.

2. Zero Tolerance for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation

Our services must not be used to facilitate, promote, request, distribute or engage in child sexual abuse or exploitation.

Prohibited conduct includes, but is not limited to:

  • Creating, requesting, uploading or distributing child sexual abuse material.
  • Sexually exploiting or abusing a child in any form.
  • Grooming, soliciting or sexually harassing minors.
  • Using our services to arrange or facilitate the abuse of a child.
  • Threatening, coercing or exploiting children through digital communications.
  • Sharing content intended to facilitate child abuse or exploitation.

Depending on the circumstances, violations may result in content removal, account restrictions, termination of access and referral to appropriate authorities where required or permitted by applicable law.

3. Child Safety Prevention Measures

We seek to implement appropriate safeguards based on the actual features, technical capabilities and risks of our services. Depending on the service, these measures may include:

  • Providing a channel for reporting child-safety concerns.
  • Reviewing credible reports and taking appropriate action.
  • Restricting access to prohibited content where applicable.
  • Taking action against accounts involved in serious violations.
  • Limiting access to sensitive information to authorised personnel.
  • Cooperating with lawful requests from competent authorities.
  • Reviewing safety procedures as our services evolve.

The safeguards implemented in practice depend on the features of the relevant service and applicable legal and platform requirements.

4. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users, parents, guardians and other concerned individuals can report suspected child exploitation, grooming, abusive content or other child-safety violations using the contact details provided below.

Where safe and appropriate, please include:

  • A brief description of the concern.
  • The relevant account name or profile, if available.
  • A link to the relevant publicly accessible content, if available.
  • The date and general circumstances of the incident.
  • Your contact details if you would like a response.

Do not download, copy, forward or attach suspected child sexual abuse material to a report. Share only information that is lawful and safe to provide.

We will assess reports based on the information available and take appropriate steps in accordance with our procedures and applicable law.

5. Handling Reports and Enforcement

Where a report raises a credible safety concern, we may take appropriate action, including:

  • Reviewing the report and relevant available information.
  • Removing or restricting access to violating content, where applicable.
  • Suspending or terminating accounts involved in serious violations.
  • Taking reasonable steps to prevent further misuse of our services.
  • Escalating concerns to law-enforcement or child-protection authorities when required or appropriate.

Reports will be handled with appropriate discretion. Access to sensitive information will be limited to authorised individuals who need it for a legitimate purpose.

6. Cooperation with Authorities

Rajpurohit Jan Seva Foundation will respond to valid legal requests and cooperate with competent law-enforcement and child-protection authorities as required by applicable law.

In India, suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation can be reported to the appropriate law-enforcement agency or child-protection authority. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

7. Child Privacy and Personal Information

We aim to handle personal information in accordance with applicable data-protection and child-protection laws. Information submitted in a safety report may be used to assess the concern, investigate it, respond appropriately, meet legal obligations and protect users.

We take reasonable steps to protect sensitive reports against unauthorised access. Information may be disclosed to competent authorities when legally required or otherwise lawfully appropriate.

For information about the collection, use, storage and deletion of personal information, please refer to the Foundation's Privacy Policy published on its official website.

8. Awareness and Responsible Use

Users must not use our services to threaten, exploit, harass or endanger children. Parents, guardians and caregivers are encouraged to help children use digital services safely and to report suspected abuse to the appropriate authorities.

Anyone who becomes aware of a credible child-safety concern should report it through the appropriate reporting channel.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our services, safety practices, technical capabilities or applicable law. The latest version, including its effective date, will be published on this page.

Users are encouraged to review this policy periodically.

10. Child Safety Contact

For child-safety reports, questions about this policy or concerns regarding the implementation of these standards, please contact Rajpurohit Jan Seva Foundation using its verified official contact details.
