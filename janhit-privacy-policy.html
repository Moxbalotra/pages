Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how information may be collected, used, stored, disclosed and deleted when you interact with Janhit Seva.

01. Introduction

Janhit Seva ("we", "our", "us") refers to Janhit Seva Sansthan and the team responsible for operating the Janhit Seva mobile application and related services.

Our platform may provide social-work updates, public notices, information about welfare initiatives, community activities, scheme information and help or contact services.

We aim to handle information in accordance with applicable Indian data-protection laws and relevant Google Play policies.

02. Information We Collect

The information we process depends on the features you use and the information you choose to provide.

A. Personal and Contact Information

  • Name and mobile number.
  • Email address and contact details, if provided.
  • Address or general location details entered by you.
  • Profile information and photograph, if uploaded.

B. Applications and Submitted Documents

If you apply for a welfare scheme, assistance programme or other service, we may process information and supporting documents you submit, where required for that specific application.

C. Technical Information

Depending on implemented technology, technical information may include device type, operating system, app version, diagnostic reports, crash logs and service usage information.

D. Information You Send Us

We may retain enquiries, feedback, complaints and other communications you voluntarily send to us.

03. App Permissions

The application may request device permissions when needed for an optional feature.

Photos and Media

Access may be requested when you choose to select and upload a profile photograph or supporting document.

Camera

If camera functionality is implemented, permission may be requested to capture a photograph or document.

Notifications

Notifications may be used for important notices, service announcements and relevant updates.

Internet Access

Network access may be used to load content, submit information and retrieve updates.

04. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage accounts, where supported.
  • To process requests, applications and enquiries.
  • To provide information about welfare schemes.
  • To send relevant service notifications.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To maintain service reliability and security.
  • To meet applicable legal obligations.

05. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary for the services described in this policy or as permitted or required by law.

Service Providers

Hosting providers, authentication providers, cloud storage services, notification services and other integrated providers may process relevant information on our behalf.

Legal and Security Reasons

Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law or valid legal process.

06. Data Security

We aim to protect personal information through appropriate technical and organisational safeguards, including access controls, secure connections and restricted administrative access where implemented.

No electronic storage or internet transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

07. Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide relevant services, fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes.

If account creation is supported, users may request account and associated data deletion through the available in-app deletion option or the designated external deletion request facility.

Contact us using the details provided below and include "Janhit Seva Account Deletion Request" in your message.

08. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate details, deletion of eligible information or withdrawal of consent where applicable.

You can contact us to exercise applicable rights. We may need to verify your identity before processing a request.

09. Children's Privacy

Janhit Seva is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Where a service is intended for children or involves children's information, appropriate safeguards, notices and parental consent will be applied where legally required.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

12. Changes to This Policy

CHILD PROTECTION POLICY

14. Child Safety Standards Policy

Janhit Seva Sansthan is committed to maintaining a safe environment and protecting children from Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE).

These Child Safety Standards apply to the Janhit Seva mobile application, its users, content and any relevant community features operated by Janhit Seva Sansthan.

We do not tolerate child sexual abuse, child exploitation, grooming or any activity that places a child at risk.

Zero-Tolerance Commitment: Any content or behaviour involving child sexual abuse or exploitation is strictly prohibited.

15. Zero Tolerance Policy Against CSAE

Janhit Seva Sansthan strictly prohibits Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).

Prohibited activities include, but are not limited to:

  • Creating, uploading, sharing or distributing CSAM.
  • Sexual exploitation or abuse of children.
  • Grooming or attempting to groom a child.
  • Sexualisation of minors.
  • Sextortion or threats involving a minor.
  • Trafficking or solicitation of children for sexual exploitation.
  • Any attempt to facilitate or encourage child abuse.

Users must not use Janhit Seva or any associated service to create, store, request, promote or distribute content that exploits or endangers children.

Violations may result in content removal, account restrictions or termination, and reporting to relevant authorities where required.

16. Reporting and User Feedback Mechanism

Users are encouraged to report any suspected child abuse, exploitation, inappropriate content or safety concern associated with Janhit Seva.

How to Report

  • Use the in-app Report, Help or Contact option to submit a child safety concern, where available.
  • Contact our designated Child Safety Contact using the email address listed in this policy.
  • Provide relevant information about the concern without downloading, forwarding or redistributing suspected illegal material.

Child Safety Reporting

Email: info@morihix.com

Subject: Child Safety Report – Janhit Seva

Reports will be reviewed by authorised personnel and handled with appropriate confidentiality.

17. Review, Investigation and Appropriate Action

When Janhit Seva Sansthan obtains actual knowledge of suspected CSAM or child sexual exploitation connected with its services, it will take appropriate action in accordance with applicable law and its published standards.

Appropriate action may include:

  • Reviewing the reported concern.
  • Restricting access to or removing prohibited content.
  • Suspending or terminating violating accounts, where applicable.
  • Preserving relevant information where legally required.
  • Reporting confirmed CSAM to the appropriate authority.
  • Cooperating with lawful investigations.

We will not knowingly allow our services to be used to distribute or facilitate child exploitation.

18. Compliance with Child Safety Laws

Janhit Seva Sansthan aims to comply with applicable child protection and online safety laws and regulations.

This includes applicable laws in India and other jurisdictions in which the application is made available.

Where legally required, confirmed CSAM incidents will be reported to the relevant regional authority or law enforcement agency.

We will cooperate with appropriate authorities in accordance with applicable legal requirements.
