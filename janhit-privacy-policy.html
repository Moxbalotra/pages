Privacy Policy

Your trust matters to us. This policy explains how Janhit Seva Sansthan handles personal information when you use our mobile application, website and related community services.

Effective: 13 October 2026 Region: India Applies to: Janhit Seva

Please read this policy carefully.

By using Janhit Seva, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to read this Privacy Policy. Where consent is legally required, we will obtain it before processing your information for the relevant purpose.

This policy should be read together with any privacy notice presented when you use a particular feature.

01. Introduction

Janhit Seva ("we", "our", "us") refers to Janhit Seva Sansthan and the team responsible for operating the Janhit Seva mobile application and related services.

Our platform may provide social-work updates, public notices, information about welfare initiatives, community activities, scheme information, and help or contact services.

This Privacy Policy describes how information may be collected, used, stored, disclosed and deleted when you interact with our services.

We aim to handle information in accordance with applicable Indian data-protection laws, Google Play policies and Apple App Store requirements.

02. Information We Collect

The information we process depends on the features you use and the information you choose to provide. We do not intend to collect information that is unnecessary for the relevant service.

A. Personal and Contact Information

Where a feature requires it, we may collect:

  • Name and mobile number.
  • Email address and contact details, if provided.
  • Address or general location details entered by you.
  • Profile information and profile photograph, if you choose to upload one.

B. Applications and Submitted Documents

If you apply for an available welfare scheme, assistance programme or other service, we may process the information and supporting documents you submit, such as identity or eligibility documents, where required for that specific application.

Certain services may involve marriage assistance, Mayara Yojana, Suraksha Sahyog Yojana or Mangal Shishu Sahyog Yojana. Information will be requested only where relevant to a service that is actually offered.

C. Technical Information

Depending on our implemented technology and configuration, we or our service providers may process technical information such as device type, operating system, app version, diagnostic reports, crash logs, approximate network information and service usage events.

D. Information You Send Us

We may retain enquiries, feedback, complaints and other communications you voluntarily send to our support team.

We do not claim to collect every category listed above in every version of the app. Actual collection depends on the features enabled and the permissions granted by you.

03. App Permissions

Janhit Seva may request certain device permissions when needed for an optional feature. Your device will display the applicable permission request.

Photos and Media

Access may be requested so you can select and upload a profile photograph or a document required for a service. We should access only the files necessary for the action you choose, using platform-supported access methods where available.

Camera

If camera functionality is implemented, permission may be requested to take a profile photograph or capture a supporting document.

Notifications

If enabled, notifications may be used for important notices, application updates, service announcements or other relevant information.

Internet Access

Network access may be used to load content, submit information you request, retrieve updates and operate online features.

We do not intend to access your camera, photos or other protected device information for unrelated purposes. You can manage available permissions through your device settings. Some features may not work if the relevant permission is denied.

04. How We Use Information

Depending on the features you use, information may be used to:

  • Create and manage your account, where accounts are supported.
  • Display your profile and user-selected information.
  • Process requests, applications and enquiries submitted by you.
  • Provide information about welfare schemes and community initiatives.
  • Send relevant service notifications and important announcements.
  • Respond to support requests and resolve technical problems.
  • Maintain service reliability, prevent fraud and protect platform security.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.
  • Improve usability and performance using permitted diagnostic or analytics information, if implemented.

We will not use personal information for a materially different purpose without an appropriate legal basis and, where required, additional notice or consent.

05. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may disclose information only when reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as permitted or required by law.

Service Providers

If integrated into the app, hosting providers, authentication providers, cloud storage services, notification services, analytics tools or crash-reporting providers may process relevant information on our behalf.

Service Delivery

Information may be shared with authorised personnel or organisations involved in delivering a service or assessing an application, where necessary and legally permitted.

Legal and Security Reasons

Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law, a valid legal process, or to address security incidents, fraud or the protection of rights and safety.

Any third-party integrations actually used by the app should be disclosed accurately. We expect service providers handling personal information on our behalf to protect it appropriately.

06. Data Security

We aim to protect personal information through appropriate technical and organisational safeguards. Depending on the system used, these may include access controls, secure connections, restricted administrative access, software updates and appropriate data-handling procedures.

No internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot promise absolute security, but we aim to reduce the risk of unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

Please keep your device and account credentials secure and contact us if you believe your account or information has been compromised.

07. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant service, fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, meet applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes and protect our legitimate interests.

Requesting Account or Data Deletion

If the app supports account creation, users can request deletion of their account and associated personal information through the account deletion option in the app or through the external deletion request facility provided by Janhit Seva.

To make a request, contact our privacy team using the details in the Contact Us section and include "Janhit Seva Account Deletion Request" in your message. Include only the information reasonably necessary to identify your account.

We will review and process valid requests within a reasonable period and in accordance with applicable law. Information that we are legally required or permitted to retain may be kept for the relevant period, with access restricted where appropriate.

Deleting an app from your device does not, by itself, delete an account or information stored on our servers.

Important for publishing: if account creation is enabled, implement a working in-app deletion flow and a publicly accessible external deletion-request webpage. Replace this general procedure with your actual process before publication.

08. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate details, deletion of eligible information, withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent, or information about how your data is handled.

You can contact us to exercise an applicable right. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Certain requests may be limited where the law permits or requires us to retain information.

You may also manage app permissions through your device settings and disable notifications through the relevant system controls.

09. Children's Privacy

Janhit Seva is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Where a service is intended for children or involves children's information, appropriate safeguards, notices and parental consent will be applied where legally required.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

10. Third-Party Services and Links

Our app or website may contain links to external websites or integrate third-party services. These services may have their own privacy policies and data practices.

We encourage you to review the privacy terms of any external service before providing personal information. Our policy applies to Janhit Seva services and does not automatically govern independent third-party websites.

Where Google Firebase, Google Play Services, Apple services or other SDKs are integrated, their actual data processing must be reflected in our applicable disclosures and store privacy declarations.

11. International Data Transfers

Depending on the hosting infrastructure and third-party providers actually used, information may be processed or stored in locations outside your state or country. Where applicable, we will follow legal requirements for cross-border transfers and use appropriate safeguards.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal obligations or data practices. The latest version will be published on this page with its revised effective date.

Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before implementing material changes.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, wish to exercise your privacy rights or want to request deletion of your account or personal information, contact Janhit Seva Sansthan.
