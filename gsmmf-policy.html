1. Introduction

Welcome to Ghanchi Siwanshi Malani Mayara Foundation. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, process and protect your information when you visit our website, use our mobile application, register as a member, participate in our activities, submit forms or make donations.

By accessing our website or using our services, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required by applicable law, we will obtain it separately.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the services you use, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Personal Information: Full name, email address, mobile number, residential address and date of birth, where required.
  • Membership Information: Membership number, registration details, application information and membership status.
  • Donation Information: Donation amount, transaction reference, payment status and payment date.
  • Communication Information: Messages, enquiries, feedback and information submitted through contact forms.
  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, device information, operating system and website usage data.
  • Voluntary Information: Documents and other information voluntarily submitted while applying for Foundation services or activities.

We collect only information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant organizational purpose.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To register and manage Foundation members.
  • To process membership applications and requests.
  • To manage donations and maintain transaction records.
  • To organize social welfare activities and community programs.
  • To communicate important announcements and updates.
  • To respond to enquiries, complaints and support requests.
  • To maintain website and application security.
  • To improve our website, mobile application and services.
  • To prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activities.
  • To comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

4. Donations and Payment Processing

Our website or mobile application may provide facilities for online donations, membership fees or other authorized payments.

Payments may be processed through third-party payment service providers, including PayPal or other payment gateways, if integrated and enabled by the Foundation.

  • Payment processing may be handled directly by the selected payment provider.
  • We may receive transaction IDs, payment confirmation, amount and payment status.
  • We do not intentionally store complete debit card or credit card details on our servers.
  • Payment providers may independently collect and process information under their own privacy policies.
  • We are not responsible for the independent privacy practices of third-party payment providers.

Users should review the privacy policy and terms of the relevant payment provider before completing a transaction.

For PayPal privacy information, please visit: PayPal Privacy Statement .

5. Membership and Registration Information

When you apply for membership or register with the Foundation, we may collect your name, contact details, address and other relevant registration information.

This information is used to verify applications, maintain membership records, issue membership confirmations and communicate relevant organizational information.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information to third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

Information may be shared in the following circumstances:

  • With authorized Foundation representatives for legitimate organizational work.
  • With payment processors for completing and verifying transactions.
  • With hosting providers and technical service providers for operating our website and application.
  • With Google services, analytics or communication tools, where integrated and applicable.
  • When required by applicable law, court orders or lawful government requests.
  • With your consent or at your direction.

These services may process certain information, including device information, usage information, identifiers or technical data, depending on the features enabled.

8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve user experience, understand website traffic, remember preferences and maintain website functionality.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, misuse or destruction.

Security measures may include access restrictions, secure hosting, password protection, encrypted connections (HTTPS) and other appropriate safeguards.

However, no internet-based transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including membership administration, donation records, legal obligations, account management and dispute resolution.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete, anonymize or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal record-retention requirements.
